Inspect HTTP requests
in real time

Spin up a unique URL, point any client at it, and watch every request stream in: headers, body, query string, timing.

Common use cases

Test webhooks

Verify what Stripe, GitHub, Slack and other providers actually send before you write the handler.

Inspect form payloads

Point your HTML form's action at a unique URL and see exactly what the browser submits.

Debug API integrations

Inspect outbound calls from third-party services or your own client libraries while you wire them up.

Mobile & IoT traffic

Point a device or microcontroller at the URL to see exactly what it puts on the wire: headers, body, every byte.

Verify cron jobs

Catch your scheduled tasks in the act and confirm they fire on time with the headers and payload you expect.

Share captures with teammates

Anyone with the URL can read the same stream. Handy for "is the payload from your side malformed, or mine?" conversations.
